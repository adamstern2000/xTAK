This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to purchases of xTAK Project software and subscriptions made through xtak.ai. It forms part of our Terms of Service & Sale.

Because xTAK products are digital software delivered immediately, we offer a 14-day refund window from the date of purchase, provided the license has not been activated on a device. Once a license has been activated, it is generally non-refundable, because activation delivers the full licensed value. If the software is materially defective and we cannot resolve the issue within a reasonable time, contact us — we will make it right, which may include a replacement or a refund.

2. Support subscriptions

You may cancel a support subscription at any time from your account or by contacting us. Cancellation stops all future renewals; it takes effect at the end of the current paid period, and you keep your subscription entitlements until then. We do not provide partial refunds for the portion of a subscription period already elapsed, except where required by law. If a renewal charges in error, contact us within 30 days and we will refund a renewal you did not intend and did not use.

3. Beta software

xTAK products are offered as Beta. Dissatisfaction with the pace of development, a specific feature, or Beta limitations is not on its own grounds for a refund outside the windows above, but we want you happy — talk to us.

4. How to request a refund

Request a refund through our contact form — include the email address used for the purchase, your order or receipt reference, and the reason for the request. We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method through our processor (Stripe); the time to appear on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer.

5. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first — we can usually resolve it faster than a bank dispute. Filing a chargeback without contacting us may result in suspension of the associated licenses pending resolution.

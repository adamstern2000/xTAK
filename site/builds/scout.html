Scout

The lightest node — a pocket-to-pack Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W running digiTAK, offline maps, and GPS for a single operator on the move. Custom-powered and sized to a compact printed case.

FEATURES

What the
Scout gives you.

  • Pocket-to-pack. The smallest node in the lineup — a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W in a compact printed case a single operator can carry anywhere.
  • digiTAK on the air. A digirig bridges an amateur radio for VHF/UHF data — run digiTAK straight from the node.
  • Offline maps. mbtileserver serves basemaps to your ATAK / WinTAK with no internet — install guide.
  • Fast GPS. A quick-fix GPS puts your position on the map and disciplines the clock — no internet, no jumper wiring.
  • Custom power. A panel-mount charge port and a tiny 5V supply keep the build sealed and field-chargeable.
  • Runs the netTAK Standalone base. Its own Wi-Fi, DNS, and DHCP so EUDs connect straight to it — no separate router.

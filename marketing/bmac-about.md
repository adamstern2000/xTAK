## xTAK — self-hosted, off-grid situational awareness for the team that has to coordinate when the WAN is gone

xTAK is a suite of seven independent services that extend the TAK ecosystem — ATAK, WinTAK, iTAK — to the places the official clients can't reach: amateur radio, LoRa mesh, off-grid Wi-Fi, SDR-decoded aircraft, browser endpoints, and on-network AI assistants.

Built for the operators who actually run things — incident commanders, EmComm operators (ARES, RACES, ACS, SATERN, MARS), SAR base teams, wildland fire, public-safety auxcomm, ham radio volunteers, county emergency managers — xTAK is designed for real deployments where the cell network is dark, TAK Server is not in the budget, and the team still needs to see each other on one shared map.

**What sets xTAK apart**

- **Off-grid by design.** No TAK Server, no cloud, no internet uplink required.
- **100% TAK protocol fidelity.** Every xTAK product joins the same local network as your ATAK and WinTAK clients — byte-identical wire output, full marker / chat / icon fidelity.
- **Stand-alone, compose by network.** Run just the products you need. They discover each other and trade data over standard TAK protocols, with no glue or integration tax.
- **Portable, pocketable, battery-powered.** baseTAK runs on a device the size of a Game Boy. chatTAK fits in a 3D-printed case smaller than a deck of cards. The whole suite fits in a backpack.
- **Self-hosted, no vendor lock-in.** Open standards, your hardware, your data, no subscription.

**The suite (Beta — shipping soon)**

- **baseTAK** — full TAK map and chat hub in a browser, with built-in offline tileserver
- **digiTAK** — amateur radio gateway: VHF/UHF, APRS-IS, and HF for continental bridging
- **loraTAK** — Meshtastic LoRa mesh gateway; every node a TAK contact
- **chatTAK** — Pi appliance with its own Wi-Fi; comms-forward TAK for non-ATAK users
- **sdrTAK** — every aircraft in the sky, every ship in the bay, on your TAK map
- **netTAK** — self-managing wireless mesh for field-deployable TAK operations
- **aiTAK** *(future release)* — local-first LLM that joins your TAK network as a peer

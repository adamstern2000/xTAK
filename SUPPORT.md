# Get xTAK & get support

**netTAK, loraTAK, and sdrTAK are in public Beta and available now.** baseTAK, digiTAK, and chatTAK are in active development; aiTAK is in the design phase. As each product is ready, it opens for Beta the same way.

🚀 **Join the public Beta → [xtak.ai/beta](https://xtak.ai/beta)**

---

## What you get

- **The software, now** — buy a device license for netTAK, loraTAK, or sdrTAK and install it on your own hardware today, at a deep Beta discount off the planned retail price.
- **Every new build as it ships** — Beta Support keeps you on the newest release for a full year. Updates land for you as they're cut.
- **3D case designs & build lists** — reference designs and printable files for operator hardware (cases, mounts, mast adapters) as they become available.
- **Direct support on Discord** — each product has its own gated support channel. A Beta purchase grants you access to that product's channel, where you can reach the team about your deployment.
- **A community of operators** — public community channels where operators deploying xTAK for real — EmComm, SAR, wildland fire, public safety, amateur radio — share what's working and swap deployment patterns.

## How to buy

Two ways to run xTAK:

1. **Install on your own hardware** — a small Linux device (Raspberry Pi 4/5, fanless mini-PC, or handheld Linux box, 4 GB+ RAM). The software runs on your network and your data stays on your hardware.
2. **Order a ready-to-deploy appliance** from us.

Either way, start at **[xtak.ai/beta](https://xtak.ai/beta)**. Installation guides are linked from each product page and pinned in the Discord `#documentation` channel.
